# Provider workflow catalog

Load only the workflow relevant to the current request.

- [emerald-exchange-market-data](../../emerald-exchange-market-data/WORKFLOW.md): Read market data and account state through the emerald-exchange read tools: list the exchange backends (`emerald_exchange_exchanges`), quote a symbol (`emerald_exchange_get_quote`), read OHLCV bars (`emerald_exchange_get_historical`), and read account balances and open positions (`emerald_exchange_get_account`, `emerald_exchange_get_positions`). Use when the agent needs a current quote, history for analysis, or equity and positions on the active backend. Do NOT use for Alpaca feed-labelled pages (use emerald-exchange-stock-reference), for FX, commodity or exchange candles (use emerald-exchange-multi-asset), or to place orders (use emerald-exchange-order-execution).
- [emerald-exchange-stock-reference](../../emerald-exchange-stock-reference/SKILL.md): Read Alpaca stock reference data with explicit labels: a feed-labelled quote (`emerald_exchange_get_stock_quote`), a bounded page of bars with adjustment and feed (`emerald_exchange_get_stock_bars`), news headlines (`emerald_exchange_get_stock_news`), dividends (`emerald_exchange_get_stock_dividends`) and FMP company profile, income, earnings or logo records (`emerald_exchange_get_company_records`). Use when a result must state its feed, adjustment, license tag and page token. Do NOT use for a plain quote or bars on any backend (use emerald-exchange-market-data) or for FX, commodities and exchange candles (use emerald-exchange-multi-asset).
- [emerald-exchange-multi-asset](../../emerald-exchange-multi-asset/SKILL.md): Read non-equity market data: bounded exchange candles from a CCXT backend (`emerald_exchange_get_exchange_history`) and provider observations for foreign exchange, commodities, spot gold and property valuation (`emerald_exchange_get_multi_asset_records`). Use when the agent needs crypto candles with a stable identity, an FX rate, a commodity bar, a gold spot price or a property valuation. Do NOT use for equities (use emerald-exchange-market-data or emerald-exchange-stock-reference) or to place orders.
- [emerald-exchange-account-intake](../../emerald-exchange-account-intake/SKILL.md): Take in account data: snapshot the Alpaca account with its positions (`emerald_exchange_get_account_snapshot`), read positions with stable identities (`emerald_exchange_get_account_positions`), and turn a statement CSV into validated activity records (`emerald_exchange_get_csv_activities`). Use when the agent must record balances and positions, reconcile a broker statement, or prepare activity for ingestion. Do NOT use for quotes and history (use emerald-exchange-market-data) or for order actions (use emerald-exchange-order-execution).
- [emerald-exchange-kg-ingestion](../../emerald-exchange-kg-ingestion/WORKFLOW.md): Make Emerald Exchange records available to the knowledge graph through the connector's declared data sources: account and position snapshots (`emerald_exchange_get_account_snapshot`, `emerald_exchange_get_account_positions`), open positions (`emerald_exchange_get_positions`), and the bounded provider pages that carry a checkpoint. Use when the agent must plan or verify what the graph receives from this connector: which tool feeds which record type, and how the checkpoint advances. Do NOT use for one-off reads that are not persisted (use emerald-exchange-market-data) or to place orders (use emerald-exchange-order-execution).
- [emerald-exchange-order-execution](../../emerald-exchange-order-execution/WORKFLOW.md): Check the state of orders and the positions they change with `emerald_exchange_get_order_status` and `emerald_exchange_get_positions`. This connector is read-only: orders are placed, changed and cancelled only through the platform's risk-guarded order tools, which check limits, halts and live-mode approval first. Use when the agent must report whether an order is open, filled or rejected, with filled quantity, average price and fees. Do NOT use to read quotes or history (use emerald-exchange-market-data) and never route an order around the risk guard.
